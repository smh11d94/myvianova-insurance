Universal life insurance gives you **permanent coverage** plus an investment account inside the policy. You decide how much to deposit and how to invest, and your growth is **sheltered from yearly tax** within the limits set by Canadian tax rules.

This flexibility makes universal life a strong fit for people who want lifelong protection but also want more say in how their money is used. When you pass away, your beneficiaries receive a **tax-free lump sum**.

## When universal life makes the most sense

Universal life suits people who have a lifelong need for coverage and room to save beyond their RRSP and TFSA. It is often used by business owners, high-income earners and families planning to pass on wealth. Because the account value is not guaranteed, it works best for people who are comfortable reviewing their policy from time to time.

## Choosing a policy

When comparing universal life policies, look closely at the details:

- **Cost of insurance:** level costs give long-term stability, while yearly renewable costs start lower but rise over time.
- **Investment options and fees:** compare the choices available and the fees charged on each account.
