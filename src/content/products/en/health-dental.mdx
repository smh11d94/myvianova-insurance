Your provincial health plan covers doctor visits and hospital stays, but many everyday health costs are not included. Individual health and dental insurance helps pay for **prescription drugs, dental care, vision care and paramedical services** when you do not have a workplace benefits plan.

It is a smart choice for self-employed people, early retirees, and anyone between jobs or leaving a company with group benefits.

## Choosing the right type of plan

There are three main types of individual plans in Canada. A **guaranteed-issue plan** has no medical questions, so anyone can qualify. An **underwritten plan** asks about your health and may offer richer coverage. A **conversion plan** lets you move from a group plan to an individual plan without medical questions, as long as you apply within the deadline after leaving.

## What to compare

When comparing health and dental plans, look at:

- **Coverage levels:** how much of each cost the plan pays and the yearly limits for drugs, dental and vision.
- **Pre-existing conditions:** whether current health issues are covered or excluded.
- **Waiting periods:** some benefits, like major dental work, may only start after a period of time.
- **Deadlines:** if you are leaving a group plan, act quickly so you do not miss your conversion window.
