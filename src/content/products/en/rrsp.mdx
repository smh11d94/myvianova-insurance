A Registered Retirement Savings Plan, or RRSP, is one of the most popular ways Canadians save for retirement. It gives you a **tax deduction today** and lets your savings **grow tax-deferred** until you need them.

The idea is simple: you put money aside while you are working and in a higher tax bracket, then take it out in retirement, when your income and tax rate are often lower.

## How much room do you have?

Each year, you earn new RRSP room equal to 18% of your previous year's earned income, up to the annual limit set by the CRA. Any room you do not use carries forward forever. Since the dollar limit changes every year, the easiest way to see your exact room is in **CRA My Account** or on your latest Notice of Assessment.

## Choosing what goes inside your RRSP

An RRSP is a container, not an investment. With us, you can fill it with insurance-based options:

- **Segregated funds:** market growth potential with guarantees on your principal at maturity or death.
- **GICs and guaranteed interest accounts:** predictable, guaranteed returns with no market risk.
- **Annuities:** when you are ready, turn your RRSP into guaranteed retirement income.

We help you choose the right mix for your goals, your timeline and your comfort with risk.
