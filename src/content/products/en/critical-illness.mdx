Critical illness insurance pays you a **tax-free lump sum** if you are diagnosed with a serious condition covered by your policy, such as cancer, a heart attack or a stroke. The money is yours to use however you need while you recover.

More Canadians now survive serious illnesses than ever before. That is good news, but recovery can take months, and the costs of lost income, travel and extra care add up quickly.

## When critical illness insurance makes the most sense

Critical illness coverage is valuable for anyone who would struggle financially if they had to stop working for a while. It is especially helpful for the self-employed, people without group benefits and families who rely on two incomes. It works well alongside life and disability insurance to round out your plan.

## Choosing a policy

When comparing critical illness policies, look beyond the price:

- **Covered conditions:** how many illnesses are covered, and how does the policy define them?
- **Partial benefits and riders:** look for early detection payouts and return of premium options.
