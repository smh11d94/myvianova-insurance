A Registered Education Savings Plan, or RESP, is the best way to save for a child's education after high school in Canada. The biggest reason is simple: **the government adds money to your savings**.

Through the Canada Education Savings Grant, the government adds 20% to the first $2,500 you contribute each year for each child. That is up to $500 a year and $7,200 per child over their lifetime, plus tax-deferred growth on all of it.

## Grants that grow your savings

The government offers two main programs for RESPs:

- **Canada Education Savings Grant (CESG):** 20% on the first $2,500 contributed each year, with catch-up of up to $1,000 a year for missed years.
- **Canada Learning Bond (CLB):** up to $2,000 for children from lower-income families, with no contribution needed.
- **Provincial programs:** some provinces offer their own grants on top of the federal ones.
