Term life insurance is the simplest and most affordable way to protect the people who depend on you. You pay a fixed premium for a set period, and if you pass away during that time, your beneficiaries receive a **tax-free lump sum**.

Because term insurance has no savings component, every dollar of premium goes toward protection. That is why a healthy 35-year-old can often get $500,000 of coverage for less than the cost of a weekly coffee.

## When term life makes the most sense

Most financial obligations are temporary: a mortgage gets paid off, children grow up, and retirement savings build over time. Term insurance is designed to cover exactly those years. Once your obligations shrink, you can reduce or drop the coverage, or convert part of it to permanent insurance for estate planning.

## Choosing a policy

When comparing term policies, look beyond the price:

- **Renewability and convertibility:** can you renew or convert without new medical evidence, and until what age?
- **Riders:** waiver of premium, child term and critical illness riders can add valuable protection.
