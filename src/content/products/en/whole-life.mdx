Whole life insurance protects you for your **entire life**, not just for a set number of years. Your premiums stay level, and your policy builds a guaranteed cash value that you can use if you need it.

Because the coverage never ends, whole life is often used to leave a legacy, pay taxes on an estate, or give a family lasting peace of mind. When you pass away, your beneficiaries receive a **tax-free lump sum**, whenever that happens.

## When whole life makes the most sense

Some needs do not go away with time. Final expenses, taxes on a cottage or investments, and the wish to leave money to family or charity are all lifelong. Whole life is designed for these needs. Many families pair it with term insurance: term covers the busy, high-debt years, and whole life covers what lasts forever.

## Choosing a policy

When comparing whole life policies, look at more than the premium:

- **Participating or not:** do you want the chance of dividends, or the certainty of fully guaranteed values?
- **Payment period:** paying over 10 or 20 years costs more each year but finishes sooner.
