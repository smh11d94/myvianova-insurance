Segregated funds let you invest in the markets while protecting part of your money with **insurance guarantees**. They work much like mutual funds, but because they are offered by insurance companies, they come with extra protection for you and your family.

Depending on the contract, **75%–100% of your deposits** are guaranteed when the contract matures, which is at least 10 years after you invest, or when you pass away. Past performance does not guarantee future returns, but the guarantee limits how much you could lose over the long term.

## When segregated funds make sense

Segregated funds are a good fit if you want growth but feel nervous about market drops, if you want your savings to pass directly to loved ones without probate, or if you own a business and want possible protection from creditors. The trade-off is higher fees, so it is important to weigh the value of the guarantees.

## What to compare

When choosing a segregated fund contract, look at:

- **Guarantee level:** 75% guarantees cost less, while 100% guarantees offer more protection for higher fees.
- **Reset features:** how often you can lock in gains and how this affects your maturity date.
- **Fees and fund choices:** compare MERs and the range of funds available.
- **Beneficiary designation:** naming the right beneficiary is key for probate savings and creditor protection.
